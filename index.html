Questions

What chemists ask first

Is it really free?

Yes. Billing, stock, udhaar, GST reports and printing are free for a single store, with no AMC. Pro features for bigger shops will be paid later.

Do I have to enter all my stock first?

No. Choose "Billing only" and bill any of the 2.4 lakh medicines today. You can import your stock from Excel later.

What if the internet goes off?

Billing keeps working. Your records are saved on your computer and go to the cloud when the internet is back.

Will it work with my printer?

Yes: A4, A5 and 2 or 3 inch thermal printers. Setup has a "Print a test bill" button to check.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Each shop's bills, stock and patients are locked to its own login. More about privacy.
