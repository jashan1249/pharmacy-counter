Getting started

How do I start?
  1. Click Start free and create your account with your shop name, mobile and email.
  2. Choose Billing only (start today, no stock) or Billing + stock.
  3. Choose your GST type and printer.
  4. Press New Bill (or F2), type a medicine name, press Enter, then Save & Print (F10).
Do I have to enter all my stock first?

No. In Billing only mode you can bill any of the 2.4 lakh medicines straight away. In Billing + stock mode, a medicine that isn't in stock can still be billed; it is marked "sold without stock".

How do I import my stock from Marg or Excel?
  1. Export your stock list from your old software to Excel.
  2. If it is an old .xls file, open it in Excel and use Save As → .xlsx (or .csv).
  3. In the app go to Medicines → Import from Excel and choose the file.
  4. Check the matched columns and the preview, then press Import.
Can I sell loose tablets?

Yes. On the bill, tap Full strip ⇄ to switch to loose tablets. Stock is reduced by the right fraction of a strip.

Printing and bills

Which printers work?

Any printer your computer can print to: normal A4 or A5 printers and 2-inch or 3-inch thermal receipt printers. Choose the size in Settings → Printer / paper.

The print window doesn't open

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Allow pop-ups for this site if your browser asks. For a shop counter, printing from a computer works best.

Can I send the bill on WhatsApp?

Yes. Enter the customer's mobile number on the bill. After saving, press Send on WhatsApp.

I made a mistake on a bill

Open the bill from Home or Reports. Use Edit bill to change it (same bill number), Return items for returns, or Cancel bill. Every change is recorded in Reports → Activity.

Your data and privacy

Where are my records kept?

On your computer first, so billing works without internet. When you are online, a copy is saved to your account in the cloud, with a daily copy kept for 14 days. If your computer breaks, log in on another one and your records come back.

Who can see my data?

Only you, with your login. Each shop's records are locked to its own account. The Pharmacy Counter team can see your shop name, contact details and how many bills you have made, so we can support you, but not your bills, stock or patients.

Can I take my data out?

Yes, anytime. Settings → Download backup gives you a complete file of your records.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes, log in with the same account. Changes sync when both are online. In the beta, avoid billing on two computers at the exact same moment; stock counts may need a quick check.
