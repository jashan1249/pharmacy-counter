Questions about price

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Billing, stock, udhaar and reports stay free. We plan to earn from Pro features for bigger shops, and from optional services like distributor ordering. We will not show ads on your bills or sell your data.

Will the free plan get limits later?

No. Features that are free today stay free for single stores.

Do I need to pay for setup?

No. You can set it up yourself in a few minutes. If you want help importing your stock or setting up your printer, message us on WhatsApp.
